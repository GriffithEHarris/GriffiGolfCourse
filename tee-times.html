How it works
Booking online

Step by step.

  1. Open the booking pagePress “Reserve online” on this page.
  2. Members: sign inEnter your household number and password. This unlocks member rates and the five-day window.
  3. Pick a time and number of playersBook for two or more golfers. Single players are not permitted.
  4. Check in when you arrivePay at the ticket office and collect your cart before heading to the first tee.

Early-morning walk-ups

No reservation? Come early.

Monday through Sunday, for the first hour and a half of play, up to 36 golfers may play the back nine without a reservation.

Walk-up places are first come, first served. Check in at the ticket office when you arrive. For same-day openings later on, call the starter.
