Advance reservations

How to reserve a tee time

Tee times are released on a rolling schedule. Members get the longest window.

  1. Go to online bookingUse the “Reserve online” button on this page.
  2. Sign in (members)Members enter their household number and password to unlock member rates and the 5-day window.
  3. Choose your time & playersBook as a twosome or larger — single players are not permitted.
  4. Check in at the ticket officePay greens fees and pick up your cart before heading to the first tee.

No reservation?

Walk-up & same-day play

Early-morning walk-ups

Monday through Sunday, for the first 1½ hours of play, up to 36 golfers may play the back nine without a reservation. First come, first served.

Same-day reservations

Open times can often be booked the same day. Check online or call the starter for current availability.

Cancellations

Plans changed? Cancel as early as possible so another group can play. Contact the starter at (203) 531-8253.
