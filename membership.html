Resident membership

Your town. Your course.

Membership is open to Greenwich residents and full-time Town of Greenwich employees. Members pay lower greens fees and can book tee times two days before the public.

Why join

What membership gives you

Lower greens fees

Adult members pay $38 for a weekday round, compared with $65 to $68 for visitors. Seniors and juniors pay even less on weekdays.

Book five days ahead

Reserve online from 6:00 a.m., five days before your round. That is two days before tee times open to the public.

Be part of the club

Join the clubs and leagues that play here, including the Griff Harris Men’s Golf Club.

Who can join

Membership is for Greenwich

Membership is open to residents of Greenwich and to full-time employees of the Town of Greenwich.

Every adult aged 25 or older in the household needs to show proof of a legal Greenwich address.

Accepted proof of address

  • A current utility bill
  • A 12-month lease
  • A Connecticut driver’s license or state ID

Already have a Greenwich beach pass? That is enough proof of residency for your golf card.

How to apply

Getting your golf card

  1. Pick up an applicationApplications are available at the golf course ticket office. Returning members receive a renewal form by email each year.
  2. Gather your documentsProof of address (or of Town employment) for each adult aged 25 and over.
  3. Return it with paymentMail your form, proof and payment, or bring them to the ticket office in person.

Juniors & young golfers

Junior memberships for ages 10 to 17 start at $75. Children aged 7 to 9 can earn a free “Pro-Certified” membership from our PGA staff, which lets them play at junior rates in the afternoons when they are with an adult.
