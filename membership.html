Resident membership

Your town. Your course.

Membership is open to Greenwich residents and full-time Town of Greenwich employees. Members enjoy reduced greens fees and a five-day booking window.

Why join

Member benefits

Lower greens fees

Adult members pay $38 on weekdays versus $65–$68 for non-members. Seniors and juniors pay even less on weekdays.

Book 5 days ahead

Reserve online starting at 6:00 a.m., five days out — two days before non-members.

Join the community

Members are eligible for the course’s clubs and leagues, including the Griff Harris Men’s Golf Club.

Eligibility

Who can join?

Membership is limited to residents of Greenwich and full-time employees of the Town of Greenwich.

Each adult age 25 and older in the household must provide proof of a legal Greenwich address.

Accepted proof of residency

  • A current utility bill
  • A 12-month lease
  • Connecticut driver’s license or state ID

Already have a Greenwich beach pass? It’s sufficient proof of residency for a golf card.

Apply & renew

Getting your card

  1. Get an applicationApplications are available at the golf course ticket office. Returning members receive a renewal form by email each year.
  2. Gather your documentsProof of residency (or Town employment) for each adult 25 and older.
  3. Submit with paymentReturn the completed form with proof and payment by mail or in person at the ticket office.

Juniors & young golfers

Junior memberships (ages 10–17) start at $75. Children ages 7–9 can earn a free “Pro-Certified” membership from the PGA staff, which lets them play at junior rates when accompanied by an adult in the afternoons.
