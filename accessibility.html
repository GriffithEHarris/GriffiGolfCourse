Accessibility

A website for every golfer

We want this website to be easy for everyone to read and use. Here is how to make it work best for you.

Make the text bigger

At the very top of every page, next to “Text size”, there are three buttons:

  • A for normal text
  • A+ for large text
  • A++ for the largest text

Try it now:

Your choice is remembered on this device. You can also zoom any web page: hold Ctrl (or ⌘ Command on a Mac) and press +.

Switch to high contrast

The “High contrast” button at the top of the page changes the colors to black and white, which can be easier to read. Press it again to switch back.

Prefer the phone?

Every phone number on this site can be tapped to call on a mobile phone. On a phone, “Call Starter” and “Book Tee Time” buttons stay at the bottom of the screen.

The starter can book tee times for you at (203) 531-8253.

Print the rates

The greens fees and membership fees have a print button that produces a clean, paper-friendly page.

Our commitment

Built to be accessible

This website is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. It uses large, clear text, strong color contrast, large buttons, and it works with a keyboard and with screen readers.

If anything on this site is hard to use, please let us know so we can fix it.
