Overview

History on King Street

The Griffith E. Harris Golf Course was designed in 1963 by renowned golf course architect Robert Trent Jones, Senior — the designer behind dozens of U.S. Open and major-championship venues.

Today it remains Greenwich’s only municipal golf course. The property includes an 18-hole par-71 course, an administration building, a full-service Pro Shop, a clubhouse and restaurant, a driving range, a putting green, a chipping green and a practice bunker.

Rules & etiquette

Course rules & regulations

A few simple rules keep The Griff fun, fair and safe for everyone.

Dress code

  • Collared or mock-neck shirts
  • Sneakers or golf shoes with non-metal spikes
  • Bathing suits or short shorts
  • Tank tops
  • Jeans

On the course

  • Twosome minimum — no single players
  • Carts follow the 90-degree rule unless “cart paths only” is posted
  • Keep cell phones silenced
  • Children under 10 are not permitted on the course, except ages 7–9 certified by the PGA staff

Safety & conduct

  • When the lightning horn sounds, stop play and seek shelter immediately
  • Alcohol is permitted only inside the clubhouse. Alcohol on the course or elsewhere on the premises results in indefinite suspension.
Pace of play

Eighteen holes in 4 h 15 m.

A full round should take four hours and fifteen minutes or less. Keep up with the group ahead, not just ahead of the group behind.
