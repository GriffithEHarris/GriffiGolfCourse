Our history

Sixty years on King Street.

Designed in 1963 by Robert Trent Jones Sr., whose work includes many of America’s great championship venues.

The Griff remains Greenwich’s only municipal golf course. Along with eighteen holes, the grounds include an administration building, a full-service Pro Shop, a clubhouse and restaurant, a driving range, a putting green, a chipping green and a practice bunker.

Course rules

A few simple rules.

They keep The Griff enjoyable, fair and safe for everyone.

What to wear

Please wear

  • A collared or mock-neck shirt
  • Sneakers, or golf shoes with non-metal spikes

Not permitted

  • Bathing suits or short shorts
  • Tank tops
  • Jeans

On the course

  • Groups of two or more; single players are not permitted
  • Carts follow the 90-degree rule unless “cart paths only” is posted
  • Please keep cell phones on silent
  • Children under 10 may not play, except ages 7 to 9 certified by our PGA staff

Safety & conduct

  • When the lightning horn sounds, stop play and take shelter right away
  • Alcohol is allowed inside the clubhouse only. Alcohol on the course or elsewhere on the grounds leads to indefinite suspension.
Pace of play

Eighteen holes in 4 hours 15 minutes

Keep up with the group ahead of you, not just ahead of the group behind. Rangers will give a friendly reminder, then a second warning, and after two warnings may ask a group to pick up and move to the next tee.
