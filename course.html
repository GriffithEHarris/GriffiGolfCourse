The Course

A Robert Trent Jones Sr. original

Opened in 1963 and owned by the Town of Greenwich, The Griff is an 18-hole, par-71 course that suits every level of golfer, from juniors learning the game to seasoned regulars.

Our history

Sixty years on King Street

The Griffith E. Harris Golf Course was designed in 1963 by the renowned architect Robert Trent Jones Sr., whose work includes many of America’s great championship venues.

It remains Greenwich’s only municipal golf course. Along with eighteen holes, the grounds include an administration building, a full-service Pro Shop, a clubhouse and restaurant, a driving range, a putting green, a chipping green and a practice bunker.

Course rules

A few simple rules

They keep The Griff enjoyable, fair and safe for everyone.

What to wear

Please wear:

  • A collared or mock-neck shirt
  • Sneakers, or golf shoes with non-metal spikes

Not permitted:

  • Bathing suits or short shorts
  • Tank tops
  • Jeans

On the course

  • Groups of two or more; single players are not permitted
  • Carts follow the 90-degree rule unless “cart paths only” is posted
  • Please keep cell phones on silent
  • Children under 10 are not permitted, except ages 7 to 9 certified by our PGA staff

Safety & conduct

  • When the lightning horn sounds, stop play and take shelter right away
  • Alcohol is allowed inside the clubhouse only. Alcohol on the course or elsewhere on the grounds leads to indefinite suspension.
Pace of play

Eighteen holes in 4 hours 15 minutes

Please keep up with the group ahead of you, not just ahead of the group behind.

Front nine: 2 h 10 min Back nine: 2 h 05 min

Total for 18 holes: 4 hours 15 minutes or less.

How our rangers help

  1. A friendly reminderRangers keep an eye on every group and will let you know if you have fallen behind.
  2. A second warningGroups that are still out of position receive a second warning.
  3. Pick up and move aheadAfter two warnings, a ranger may ask the group to pick up and move to the next tee.
