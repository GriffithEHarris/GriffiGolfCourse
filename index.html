The course

A Robert Trent Jones Sr. classic, kept for the public.

Designed in 1963 by one of golf’s most celebrated architects, The Griff is Greenwich’s only municipal course — a full 18 holes of tree-lined fairways, well-placed bunkers and true greens.

  • 18-hole, par-71 layout with four sets of tees
  • Full-service Pro Shop and clubhouse restaurant
  • Driving range, putting & chipping greens, practice bunker
  • Back-nine walk-up play for early risers

Booking windows

When can I book?

All advance reservations are made online. Members need their household number and password.

5days ahead

Members

Online booking opens at 6:00 a.m., up to five days before your round.

3days ahead

Non-members

Visitors and guests may reserve online up to three days in advance.

36walk-up spots

Early-morning back nine

For the first 1½ hours of play, up to 36 golfers may walk up to the back nine — no reservation needed.

Questions about today’s tee sheet? Call the starter at (203) 531-8253.
