Welcome

Greenwich’s course, since 1963

Along King Street, The Griff has been the Town of Greenwich’s public golf course for more than sixty years. Robert Trent Jones Sr., the architect behind many of America’s great championship courses, laid out its eighteen holes, and the Town has looked after them ever since.

Whether you are a longtime member who knows every break on the greens, a family bringing a grandchild out for the first time, or a visitor passing through Fairfield County, you are welcome here.

Good to know

Common questions

Simple answers to the questions we hear most often at the starter’s booth.

Do I have to live in Greenwich to play?

No. The Griff is a public course and everyone is welcome. Visitors can reserve a tee time online up to three days ahead. Membership, which brings lower greens fees and a longer booking window, is for Greenwich residents and full-time Town employees.

How do I book a tee time?

Reserve online through our tee times page. If you would rather speak with someone, call the starter at (203) 531-8253.

Can I play without a reservation?

Yes, early in the day. For the first hour and a half of play each morning, Monday through Sunday, up to 36 golfers may walk up and play the back nine without a reservation.

Can I play on my own?

The course has a twosome minimum, so please book with at least one other golfer. Single players are not permitted.

Is there a senior rate?

Yes. Senior members (age 65 and over) pay $28 for 18 holes on weekdays. A 2026 senior membership is $110 when purchased before May 1. See membership fees.

Are golf carts available?

Yes. A two-person cart is $40 for 18 holes or $25 for 9 holes, and a single-rider cart is $27. Carts follow the 90-degree rule unless “cart paths only” is posted. Cart and range prices.

What should I wear?

Collared or mock-neck shirts, with sneakers or golf shoes with non-metal spikes. Bathing suits, short shorts, tank tops and jeans are not permitted.

Can I bring my grandchildren?

Junior memberships are available for ages 10 to 17. Children under 10 are not permitted on the course, except children ages 7 to 9 who have been certified by our PGA staff. Learn about junior golf.

How long does a round take?

We ask that 18 holes take 4 hours and 15 minutes or less: about 2 hours 10 minutes for the front nine and 2 hours 5 minutes for the back.

What happens if there is lightning?

When the lightning horn sounds, stop play immediately and seek shelter.

Still have a question? We are happy to help.
