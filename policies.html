Cancellations & no-shows

Need to cancel?

Please cancel as early as you can, so another group can enjoy the time.

  • 48 hours’ notice. Cancel at least 48 hours before your tee time. After that, a no-show fee is charged.
  • Weekends and holidays. Tee times generally can’t be cancelled unless the course is closed for weather.
  • Rain, but the course is open? You can cancel a prepaid tee time that day for a refund.

How to cancel

Call the starter. Have the name the tee time was booked under ready.

Rain checks

When the weather cuts it short.

A rain check lets you come back and finish another day.

You can get a rain check when

  • The course closes
  • It rains for 30 minutes and you choose to stop
  • The greens flood
  • Play is stopped for lightning

Frost delays

Frost delays don’t push times back.

Walking on frosted grass damages it, so on cold mornings play starts later. Tee times before the delay ends are cancelled, and play begins with the first tee time after it lifts.

For example: if the frost delay lasts until 10:00 a.m., play begins with the 10:00 a.m. tee time. Every tee time before 10:00 a.m. is cancelled.
