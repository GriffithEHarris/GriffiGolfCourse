Ask the starter

Questions? We’ve heard them all.

And we are always happy to answer them again, in person or on the phone.

(203) 531-8253
Do I have to live in Greenwich to play?

No. The Griff is a public course and everyone is welcome. Visitors can book online up to three days ahead. Membership, with lower fees, is for Greenwich residents and full-time Town employees.

Can I play without a reservation?

Yes, early in the day. For the first hour and a half of play each morning, up to 36 golfers may walk up and play the back nine.

Can I play on my own?

The course has a twosome minimum, so please come with at least one other golfer. Single players are not permitted.

Is there a senior rate?

Yes. Senior members (65 and over) pay $28 for a weekday round, and a 2026 senior membership is $110 before May 1.

What should I wear?

A collared or mock-neck shirt, with sneakers or golf shoes with non-metal spikes. No jeans, tank tops, short shorts or bathing suits.

Can I bring my grandchildren?

Junior memberships are for ages 10 to 17. Children under 10 may not play, except ages 7 to 9 certified by our PGA staff.

What if there’s lightning?

When the lightning horn sounds, stop play right away and take shelter.
